> [!IMPORTANT]
> ✉️ **A Personal Message**
> 
> Hey, I just wanted to welcome you personally! This is Frequent Habits—the habit tracker I built after searching high and low for an app with the exact feature set I wanted. As you can probably guess, I never found one. But I’m actually glad I didn't! It gave me the chance to build my very first app, and it has been an absolute blast.
> 
> Frequent Habits has everything I always wished for: an ultra-clean design, deep analytics (so I can really dive into all the quirks of my behavior), an interactive widget, local exports so I don't have to sync personal data to Big Tech, and gamification to keep me motivated!
> 
> I hope you enjoy using it as much as I loved creating it. If you have any feedback or feature requests, feel free to reach out via [Issues](https://github.com/FrequeNCy144/Frequent-Habits/issues/new/choose) or through the email address in the app—I’m always open to suggestions, though I can’t promise every feature will make it in!
> 
> Enjoy! ❤️  
> @FrequeNCy144

🎯 ** Ziel-Flexibilität (Track at Your Frequency) – Flexible Targets, rollierende Zeitfenster & Farbordner

📊 **Reichhaltige Statistiken (Unrivaled Statistics) – Jährliche Heatmaps, Discipline-Scores & Trends

📝 **Obsidian Daily Notes Sync – Automatischer Export von Callout-Boxen & Statistiken in dein Obsidian Vault

☁️ **WebDAV Cloud Sync – Privater Abgleich über deinen eigenen Nextcloud-, Synology- oder WebDAV-Server

🏆 **Meilensteine & Belohnungen (Milestone Rewards) – Streak-Badges & echte persönliche Belohnungen

📜 **Tagebuch & Zeitkapsel (Reflect Your Journey) – Tägliche Reflexionen & Briefe an dein zukünftiges Ich

🧠 **Habit-Psychologie – Habit Stacking, Minimum-Viable-Mode & strukturierte Routinen

🎧 **In-App Fokus-Timer – Pomodoro mit 12 Naturklängen & eigenem Audio-Import

💡 **Algorithmetische Smart Insights – Muster- und Routine-Analysen

⚡ **Homescreen Widgets – 1-Tap Schnell-Abhaken direkt vom Startbildschirm

🎨 **Eigener Style – AMOLED True Black, Clean Light Mode, E-Ink Papier-Theme & Farbakzente

📥 **1-Klick Import & JSON Backup – Nahtloser Umstieg von anderen Trackern & volle Datensouveränität

🔒 **100% Privat & Werbefrei – Null Tracking, keine Registrierung, standardmäßig Offline-First

## 🚀 Installation

You can install and update **Frequent Habits** in three convenient ways:

1. **[F-Droid Store](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.frequent.habits/) (Recommended):** Download directly via F-Droid for seamless background updates.
2. **[Obtainium](https://github.com/ImranR98/Obtainium):** Use the Obtainium badge above or paste our GitHub repository URL into Obtainium for direct release notifications and OTA updates.
3. **Manual APK:** Download the latest `FrequentHabits.apk` directly from our [Releases Page](https://github.com/FrequeNCy144/Frequent-Habits/releases) and install it manually.
